Welcome to this edition of CODA Countdown, our monthly update designed to keep the DCG community informed about our progress toward the upcoming Commission on Dental Accreditation (CODA) accreditation visit.

Accreditation preparation is a collaborative effort that involves faculty, staff, residents, students, and administrators across all programs. Each milestone completed brings us one step closer to demonstrating the strength of our educational programs and our ongoing commitment to continuous improvement.

    Action item of the month

    Keep documentation current

    Accurate and up to date documentation is an important part of demonstrating our compliance with CODA standards. As we continue preparing for the accreditation visit, every member of the DCG community contributes to our readiness by ensuring that the materials supporting our programs accurately reflect current practices. Small updates made now can help prevent inconsistencies later and ensure our self-study documents, evidence, and daily operations tell the same story.
